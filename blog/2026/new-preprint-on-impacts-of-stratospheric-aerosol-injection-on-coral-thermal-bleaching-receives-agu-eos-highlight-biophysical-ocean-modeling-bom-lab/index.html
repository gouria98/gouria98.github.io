Biophysical Ocean Modeling (BOM) LabDepartment of Ocean and Coastal Science, Louisiana State UniversityResearch article led by PhD Candidate Gouri Anil titled “Impacts of Timing and Level of Stratospheric Aerosol Injection on Coral Thermal Bleaching” out now. The work received attention from the American Geophysical Union magazine Eos ahead of AGU 2025 Fall Meeting held in New Orleans, where Gouri presented her work. Read the Eos article here. Frequent and severe coral bleaching has resulted in widespread coral mortality and decline across the globe. Solar climate intervention (SCI) is being considered as a supplement to emission reductions and other risk mitigation strategies to prevent exceeding climate tipping points. The work uses daily sea surface temperature data from the Community Earth System Model simulations to project and compare thermal coral bleaching under a moderate emissions scenario, SSP2-4.5, with and without intervention using stratospheric aerosol injection (SAI). The study uses three SAI scenarios: SAI-1.0, starting in 2035 and limiting warming to 1.0℃, SAI-1.5, starting in 2035 and limiting warming to 1.5℃, and SAI-Delayed starting in 2045 and limiting warming to ~1.37℃ to compare the effects of timing and intensity of SAI injections on thermal bleaching. The study found that under all three SAI scenarios, global reef provinces experience significantly lower levels of thermal stress relative to SSP2-4.5. Among the three intervention scenarios, SAI limiting warming to 1.0℃ in 2035 most effectively mitigates thermal bleaching over space and time.The work is a part of Gouri Anil’s dissertation work and aims to inform reef managers, ecologists and policymakers on the potential benefits and risks of implementing SAI.
